Safety Considerations & Who Should Be Careful

Even when a supplement contains ingredients commonly used in wellness products, safety should not be assumed automatically. Botanical compounds and cognitive-support ingredients can have biological effects and may interact with medicines or other supplements.

  • Review the complete label: Check the current ingredient list and serving information, especially if you have known allergies or sensitivities.
  • Consider medication interactions: People using prescription medicines should discuss new supplements with a healthcare professional when appropriate because some ingredients may affect how medicines work.
  • Existing health conditions: A dietary supplement may not be suitable for everyone with a medical condition, even when its ingredients are plant-based or naturally derived.
  • Pregnancy and nursing: Anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding should obtain appropriate professional guidance before using a dietary supplement.
  • Unexpected reactions: Stop using the product and seek appropriate medical advice if you experience a significant or unusual reaction.
  • Persistent cognitive concerns: Ongoing or worsening memory, confusion, personality changes, difficulty performing familiar tasks, or other concerning symptoms should be evaluated professionally rather than self-treated with supplements.
